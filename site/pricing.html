Pricing FAQ

The fine print, in plain English.

Still unsure? Email us.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every account starts with a 14-day free trial when we launch. You won’t be charged until it ends, and you can cancel before then.

Do I need a card to join early access?

No. Joining early access only needs your email address.

What fees will I pay on card payments?

CrumbQuote takes no commission. Card payments are processed by Stripe, which charges its standard processing fee per payment. Check Stripe’s pricing page for the rate in your country.

How long does the founding price last?

For as long as you stay subscribed. If you cancel and come back later, the regular price applies.

Is there a yearly option?

Yes. The yearly plan is $90, which works out to two months free.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts. Cancel from your settings and you won’t be charged again.
