Deposits & payments

Get paid before you start baking.

Customers pay a deposit by card when they book. The balance is due before pickup, and reminders go out automatically.

  • Set your deposit: a percentage or a fixed amount
  • Card payments processed securely by Stripe
  • Your cancellation and refund policy shown at checkout
  • No commission from us. Stripe’s standard fee applies

The details

Small things that make a big difference.

Made for phones

Customers can order from their phone, and you can manage every order from yours, right in the kitchen.

Quote on request

For one-off designs, customers send photos and details, and you reply with a price they can accept and pay.

Your customers stay yours

We never market to your customers or show them other bakers. Export your list anytime.

Pickup or delivery

Offer pickup windows, local delivery with a fee, or both, and set the area you deliver to.

Flexible menus

Price by serving, by the dozen, by the box or by the piece, with add-ons for everything else.

Secure by default

Card details are handled by Stripe and never touch our servers.
