FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Email us and a real person will reply.

When can I start using CrumbQuote?

We’re building it now. Early access members get in first, in small groups, so we can help every baker set up properly.

Do I need a website?

No. You get your own order page with a link to share anywhere. If you already have a website, just link to it.

What about cakes that are too custom for a set price?

Mark any option as “quote on request”. The customer sends details and inspiration photos, and you reply with a price from inside CrumbQuote.

Does it work for cookies, cupcakes and other treats?

Yes. Price by the dozen, by the box or by the piece.

Does CrumbQuote take a cut of my sales?

No. You pay the flat monthly plan. Card payments are processed by Stripe, which charges its standard processing fee.

Is selling from home allowed where I live?

Rules for home food businesses differ by state and country, and we can’t give legal advice. Check your local cottage food rules before you take orders.
