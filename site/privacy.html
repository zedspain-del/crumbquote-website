Privacy policy

Plain-English details about the information you share when you join CrumbQuote early access.

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you join early access, we collect your email address and any optional answers you give about your baking business: what you bake, how you take orders, roughly how many orders you get, and anything you write in the comment box.

Why we collect it

  • To tell you when your CrumbQuote access is ready.
  • To understand what bakers need, so we build the right product.

We only email you about CrumbQuote, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Where it’s stored

Sign-ups are stored by our website host, Netlify, using its form service. We don’t sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Cookies and third parties

This site doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers to download them.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email you@example.com and we’ll take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. When CrumbQuote launches, we’ll publish a full policy covering the product itself.
