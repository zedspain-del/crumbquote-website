About

Helping small bakers get paid fairly.

CrumbQuote is a new, independent product built for home and cottage bakers who take custom orders.

Why we’re building CrumbQuote

Custom bakers do skilled, creative work. Yet many spend their evenings answering the same price questions, doing cost math on the back of an envelope, and chasing deposits from people who may never pick up.

The tools that exist tend to be either general-purpose shop builders that don’t understand custom cakes, or software made for bigger bakeries with bigger budgets.

We think a baker should be able to set fair prices once, share one link, and let the order page handle the rest: the price, the deposit, the calendar and the reminders. That’s CrumbQuote.

What we believe

The principles behind the product.

Fair, flat pricing

One low monthly price and no commission. Your busiest month should be your best month, not our biggest cut.

Bakers first

Founding bakers shape what we build. If it doesn’t save you time in a real kitchen, it doesn’t ship.

Your customers are yours

We never market to your customers, show them other bakers, or sell their data. Export everything, anytime.

Simple over clever

You should be able to set up your menu and prices in an evening, without a manual.
